Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

Operating Entity: MSC Case Monitor is operated by Joel Alvarado, U.S. Sole Proprietor. Joel Alvarado is the individual legally responsible for the operation of MSC Case Monitor and the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how MSC Case Monitor collects, uses, stores, protects, shares, retains, and deletes information.

MSC Case Monitor is an independent software application designed to retrieve, organize, and monitor case-status information made available through authorized U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) API services.

MSC Case Monitor is not affiliated with, endorsed by, operated by, or part of USCIS or the U.S. Department of Homeland Security.

2. Information We Collect or Process

MSC Case Monitor follows a data-minimization approach. We process only information reasonably necessary to provide case-monitoring functionality.

The application may process:

  • USCIS receipt numbers voluntarily provided for case monitoring.
  • Case-status information returned by authorized USCIS API services.
  • Case-status dates, history, and technical metadata returned by USCIS.
  • Technical logs needed for security, diagnostics, reliability, and troubleshooting.
  • Contact information voluntarily provided when a user requests support, access, correction, or deletion.

MSC Case Monitor does not intentionally collect payment card information, bank information, medical information, genetic information, family medical history, precise geolocation, device contacts, or biometric information as part of its case-monitoring functionality.

Users should not submit medical, genetic, family-history, financial, or other unrelated sensitive information to MSC Case Monitor.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Retrieve authorized USCIS case-status information.
  • Detect changes in case-status information over time.
  • Maintain case-status history when that feature is enabled.
  • Provide monitoring or notifications requested by a user.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.
  • Investigate technical errors or service failures.
  • Respond to user support and privacy requests.

Information will not be used for a materially different purpose without appropriate notice and active consent when consent is required.

4. Sale of Personal Information

MSC Case Monitor does not sell personal information.

Personal information is not exchanged for money, advertising revenue, data-broker services, or other monetary consideration.

5. Third Parties and Service Providers

MSC Case Monitor does not share personal information with advertisers, marketers, or data brokers.

The public informational website for MSC Case Monitor is currently hosted using GitHub Pages. Case-monitoring receipt numbers and USCIS case-status records are not intentionally published to or stored in the public GitHub Pages repository.

Personal information may be disclosed to a technical service provider only when that disclosure is necessary to operate the service and appropriate privacy and security protections are in place.

Before a new service provider is permitted to receive personal information, MSC Case Monitor will require that provider to protect the information and restrict its use to the purpose for which it was provided.

Personal information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, court order, or another valid legal requirement.

6. Third-Party Use and Active Consent

A third party may not independently use or disclose personal information received from MSC Case Monitor for an unrelated purpose without the user's active consent.

This restriction also applies to information that has been de-identified, anonymized, or pseudonymized when that information originated from MSC Case Monitor user data.

Any third party permitted to process information on behalf of MSC Case Monitor must be bound by privacy and security obligations consistent with the commitments described in this Privacy Policy.

7. De-Identified, Anonymized, and Aggregated Information

MSC Case Monitor may use aggregated, anonymized, pseudonymized, or de-identified information for testing, system reliability, analytics, security, or performance improvement.

Reasonable measures will be used to prevent such information from being used to identify a person.

De-identified, anonymized, pseudonymized, or aggregated information will not be sold.

8. Data-Sharing Choices

Users control whether they provide a USCIS receipt number or other information to MSC Case Monitor.

If a future feature allows information to be shared with another person or service, users will be informed of the purpose of the sharing and any material risks, benefits, or limitations.

When active consent is required, information will not be shared until the user takes an affirmative action agreeing to that specific disclosure.

Users may withdraw consent for future sharing where applicable. Withdrawal will not necessarily reverse a disclosure that was already lawfully completed.

9. Impact of Sharing Information About Others

Information concerning another person may affect that person's privacy. Users should provide only case information they are authorized to use.

MSC Case Monitor does not intentionally collect genetic information, family medical history, or medical information. Users should not submit such information about themselves or another person.

10. Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, destruction, or loss.

USCIS API credentials, OAuth credentials, production keys, client secrets, and similar authentication information are stored separately from publicly accessible application code.

Authentication secrets are not intentionally published in the public website or public source-code repository.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. MSC Case Monitor therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested functionality, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or support legitimate operational needs.

Case-monitoring information may be retained while monitoring for that case remains active.

If no monitoring activity occurs for 12 consecutive months, associated case-monitoring information will be deleted or de-identified within 30 days after that inactivity period, unless a longer period is required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, or an unresolved legal matter.

Technical security logs may be retained only for the period reasonably necessary for security, diagnostics, or legal compliance.

12. Permanent Data Deletion

Users may request permanent deletion of personal information associated with MSC Case Monitor at any time.

To request deletion, send an email to:

Privacy and deletion requests:
msccasemonitor@gmail.com

After a valid request is verified, applicable personal information will be deleted from active systems within 30 days, unless retention is required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, or an active dispute.

Information in protected backups may remain for a limited additional period until those backups are securely rotated or deleted.

13. Closing or Discontinuing Use

A user may stop using MSC Case Monitor at any time.

A user may request closure of any persistent monitoring relationship and deletion of associated personal information by emailing msccasemonitor@gmail.com .

14. Data Breach Notification

If MSC Case Monitor becomes aware of a security incident involving personal information, affected users will be notified when required by applicable law.

When appropriate, the notification will explain the nature of the incident and provide instructions or recommended actions users may take to protect themselves.

15. California Privacy Rights

To the extent the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended, applies to MSC Case Monitor, eligible California residents may exercise applicable rights to know, access, correct, or delete personal information.

MSC Case Monitor does not sell personal information.

Requests may be submitted to msccasemonitor@gmail.com .

16. Transfer of Ownership

If MSC Case Monitor is transferred to another owner or operating entity, users will be notified when required by applicable law.

The successor will be required to protect personal information in a manner consistent with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

If a successor intends to use personal information in a materially different way, users will be given appropriate notice and, where required, the ability to provide active consent or request deletion before the different use occurs.

If MSC Case Monitor permanently ends operations, personal information will be securely deleted or de-identified unless retention is required by law.

MSC Case Monitor does not collect health information as part of its intended service. If a future version ever processes health information, users will be given an appropriate method to securely delete, transmit, or download that information before a transfer or discontinuation when required.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality, legal requirements, security practices, or data practices change.

Material changes will be communicated in clear language.

A plain-language summary of material changes will be provided when appropriate.

When a material change requires active consent, MSC Case Monitor will ask the user to take an affirmative action accepting that change before the affected processing begins.

Silence, inactivity, or continued use alone will not be treated as active consent when active consent is legally required.

The effective date shown at the top of this policy will be updated when the policy changes.

18. Immigration and Legal Disclaimer

MSC Case Monitor provides software-based case-status monitoring functionality only.

It does not provide legal advice, immigration representation, or a guarantee regarding USCIS processing or case outcomes.

Users should confirm official case information directly with USCIS and consult a qualified attorney or accredited representative when legal advice is needed.
