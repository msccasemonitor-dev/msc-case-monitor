Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

Operating Entity: MSC Case Monitor is operated by Joel Alvarado, U.S. Sole Proprietor. Joel Alvarado is the individual legally responsible for the operation of MSC Case Monitor and the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how MSC Case Monitor collects, uses, stores, protects, shares, retains, and deletes information associated with use of the application.

MSC Case Monitor is an independent software application designed to retrieve, organize, and monitor case-status information made available through authorized U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) API services.

MSC Case Monitor is not affiliated with, endorsed by, operated by, or part of USCIS or the U.S. Department of Homeland Security.

2. Information We May Collect

MSC Case Monitor follows a data-minimization approach and collects only information reasonably necessary to provide case-monitoring functionality.

Information processed by the application may include:

  • USCIS receipt numbers voluntarily provided for case monitoring.
  • Case-status information returned by authorized USCIS API services.
  • Case-status dates, history, and related metadata returned by the API.
  • Technical application logs necessary for security, diagnostics, reliability, and troubleshooting.
  • Contact information voluntarily provided by a user when requesting support, exercising privacy rights, or requesting deletion.

MSC Case Monitor does not intentionally collect financial account information, payment card data, medical information, precise geolocation information, contacts from a user's device, or biometric information as part of its case-monitoring functionality.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Retrieve authorized USCIS case-status information.
  • Detect changes in case-status information over time.
  • Maintain case-status history when that functionality is enabled.
  • Provide notifications or monitoring features requested by a user.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.
  • Investigate technical errors or service failures.
  • Respond to user support or privacy requests.

Information will not be used for purposes that are materially unrelated to MSC Case Monitor's stated functionality without providing appropriate notice and obtaining consent where required.

4. Sale of Personal Information

MSC Case Monitor does not sell personal information.

Personal information is not exchanged with third parties for money, advertising revenue, data brokerage, or other monetary consideration.

5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

MSC Case Monitor does not share personal information with marketers or data brokers.

Information may be disclosed only when reasonably necessary:

  • To service providers performing technical services necessary to operate the application, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.
  • To comply with applicable law, regulation, court order, or valid governmental request.
  • To protect the security, integrity, or legal rights of MSC Case Monitor, its operator, or its users.
  • When a user has provided affirmative consent for a specific disclosure.

Third parties are not authorized to independently use or disclose personal information received from MSC Case Monitor for unrelated purposes.

6. De-Identified and Aggregated Information

If MSC Case Monitor uses aggregated, anonymized, pseudonymized, or de-identified information for testing, analytics, performance monitoring, or system improvement, reasonable measures will be used to prevent that information from being used to identify an individual.

Such information will not be sold.

7. Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, destruction, or loss.

USCIS API credentials, OAuth credentials, production keys, and similar authentication secrets are stored separately from publicly accessible application source code and are not intentionally exposed to users.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, MSC Case Monitor cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested functionality, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or support legitimate operational needs.

Case-monitoring information that is no longer required for the requested service will be deleted or de-identified within a reasonable period unless retention is required by law.

Information associated with inactive or dormant use will not be kept indefinitely when there is no legitimate operational or legal need for continued retention.

9. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request permanent deletion of personal information associated with MSC Case Monitor.

After verifying a legitimate deletion request, MSC Case Monitor will delete applicable personal information from active systems within 30 days, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law or necessary for security, fraud prevention, or the resolution of an active legal matter.

Information contained in protected backup systems may remain for a limited additional period until those backups are securely rotated or deleted.

Privacy or deletion requests:
Email: msccasemonitor@gmail.com

10. User Choices and Consent

Users control whether they provide a USCIS receipt number or other information to MSC Case Monitor.

When affirmative consent is legally required for a new use or disclosure of personal information, MSC Case Monitor will request that consent before proceeding.

Users may discontinue use of MSC Case Monitor and request deletion of their information as described above.

11. Data Breach Notification

If MSC Case Monitor becomes aware of a security incident involving personal information, affected users will be notified when required by applicable law.

When appropriate, such notification will describe the incident and provide recommended actions that affected users may take to protect themselves.

12. California Privacy Rights

To the extent the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended, applies to MSC Case Monitor, applicable California residents may exercise rights provided under that law, including applicable rights to know, access, correct, or delete personal information.

MSC Case Monitor does not sell personal information.

13. Transfer or Discontinuation of the Application

If MSC Case Monitor is transferred to another owner, users will be informed when required by applicable law.

A successor operator will be required to protect personal information consistently with applicable law and the commitments made in this Privacy Policy, or users will be provided an appropriate opportunity to delete or otherwise control their information before a materially different use occurs.

If MSC Case Monitor permanently ceases operations, personal information will be securely deleted, de-identified, or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality, legal requirements, or data practices change.

Material changes will be clearly communicated. When affirmative consent is required by law for a materially different use of personal information, consent will be obtained before that new use occurs.

Updated policies will include a revised effective date and a plain-language description of material changes when appropriate.

15. Immigration and Legal Disclaimer

MSC Case Monitor provides software-based case-status monitoring functionality only. It does not provide legal advice, immigration representation, or a guarantee regarding USCIS processing or case outcomes.

Users should confirm official case information directly with USCIS and consult a qualified attorney or accredited representative when legal advice is needed.
