About MSC Case Monitor

MSC Case Monitor is an independently developed software project intended to help users monitor USCIS case-status information and identify changes in information returned through authorized USCIS services.

The application is operated by Joel Alvarado, U.S. Sole Proprietor.

Data and Security

MSC Case Monitor is designed to minimize the collection and retention of personal information. Application credentials and authentication information are intended to be stored securely and separately from publicly accessible application code.

Additional information about data handling, retention, deletion, and user rights is available in our Privacy Policy.

Independent Service

MSC Case Monitor is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) or the U.S. Department of Homeland Security.

Official immigration case information should always be confirmed directly through USCIS.
